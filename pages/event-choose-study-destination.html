How to Choose the Right Study Destination

A demo webinar detail page for students comparing destination, cost, lifestyle and long-term opportunity.

Home/Resources/Events & Webinars/Webinar
18 Sep 2026

Session overview

This webinar helps students understand how to compare study destinations based on academic goals, budget, preferred lifestyle, intakes, family plans and post-study opportunities.

What the session covers

The session can include destination comparisons, intake planning, cost considerations, documentation readiness and a guided Q&A with a Range Global Education counselor.

Who should attend

Students and families who are still deciding between study destinations and want clearer guidance before choosing a pathway.
