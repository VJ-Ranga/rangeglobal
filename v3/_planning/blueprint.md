# V3 Website Blueprint

## Global Layout

### Header

- Logo on the left
- Primary navigation on desktop
- Mobile menu on smaller screens
- Main CTA: `Contact Us` or `Start Your Journey`
- Resource pages grouped under `Resources`

### Footer

- Short brand statement using approved positioning
- Quick links
- Student pathway links
- University partnership links
- Contact area marked as waiting on client details
- Copyright line

### Shared CTA Pattern

- Primary CTA: connect with Range Global Education
- Secondary CTA: explore student services or partnerships
- Avoid claims not found in DOCX

### 4. Audience Split Section

- Items: Direct Collaborations, International Recruitment, Market Representation, Student Outreach, Partnership Development

- Items: Destination Selection, Programme Guidance, Admission Process, Visa Support, Pre-Departure Preparation

### 5. Our Strengths & Global Reach

- Explain multiple education systems and coordinated admission process

### 6. Study Destinations Preview

- Show the eight approved destinations only
- CTA: `View Study Destinations`

### 7. University Partnerships Preview

- Show direct collaborations grouped by Malta, New Zealand, Georgia, Malaysia
- Include global recruitment partnerships separately
- CTA: `View University Partnerships`

### 8. Complete Support Services Preview

- Show the 10-step journey as an ordered process
- CTA: `View Student Services`

### 9. Testimonials Preview

- Show four categories only: Student stories, Parent testimonials, University partner feedback, Video testimonials
- No names, quotes, outcomes, or invented stories

### 10. Final CTA

- Invite users to connect with RGE
- Mention missing contact details as a build-time placeholder only until client confirms
